Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 25, 2026

Wallpaper World respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information when you use the application.

Information Collection

Wallpaper World does not directly collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, or phone number.

Internet Access

The app uses an internet connection to load and display wallpapers through the Pexels API.

Permissions

The app may request photo or storage-related permissions when you choose to download wallpapers to your device. These permissions are used only to allow the app to save downloaded wallpapers to your device.

Favorites

Your favorite wallpapers are stored locally on your device using SharedPreferences. Favorite information is not uploaded to or stored on our servers.

Third-Party Services

Wallpaper World uses third-party services to provide wallpapers and display advertisements.

Pexels API:
The app uses the Pexels API to load and display wallpapers. Pexels may process information according to its own privacy practices.

Google AdMob:
The app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may collect and use information such as advertising identifiers, device information, and information about ad interactions for advertising, measurement, personalization, and related purposes, subject to applicable settings and policies.

For more information about how Google handles information, please review Google's Privacy Policy .

Children's Privacy

Wallpaper World is not directed toward children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Security

Wallpaper World does not operate its own server for storing users' personal information. Information stored locally by the app, such as favorite wallpapers, remains on the user's device.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated effective date.

Contact

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or Wallpaper World, please contact the developer through the contact information associated with the application.
