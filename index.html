Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 25, 2026

Wallpaper World respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the app collects and uses information.

Information Collection

Wallpaper World does not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, or phone number.

Internet Access

The app uses an internet connection to load wallpapers from the Pexels API.

Permissions

The app requests storage/photo permissions only when you choose to download wallpapers to your device.

Favorites

Your favorite wallpapers are stored locally on your device using SharedPreferences. This information is never uploaded to any server.

Third-Party Services

This app uses the Pexels API to provide wallpapers. Please review the Pexels Privacy Policy for more information.

Children's Privacy

The app is not directed toward children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time.

Contact

If you have any questions, please contact the developer.
